# Honorables: Bird's-Eye View

At the highest level, **Honorables is not a survival modpack—it is a civilization simulator built on top of Minecraft.**

The server revolves around a single central principle:

> **Players do not become powerful by finding better loot. They become powerful by building better civilization.**

Everything else exists to reinforce that philosophy.

---

# 1. Core Design Pillars

## Deterministic Progression

Progression should rarely depend on RNG.

Instead, advancement comes from:

* exploration
* specialization
* infrastructure
* player knowledge
* industrial capability
* regional access
* craftsmanship

Luck may influence opportunities but should never replace investment.

---

## Civilization Over Individualism

The primary long-term progression object is **the settlement**, not the player.

Players improve:

* workshops
* foundries
* farms
* libraries
* magical facilities
* transportation
* resource networks

The settlement enables everyone to reach greater heights.

---

## Player Economy

There is **no explicit currency**.

Trade occurs through:

* services
* specialization
* materials
* crafted goods
* negotiated exchange

The economy emerges naturally from scarcity and expertise.

---

# 2. Regional Quality Framework

The world is divided into hidden Voronoi-style regions.

Each region possesses deterministic quality profiles.

```
Dimension
        ↓
Voronoi Capital
        ↓
Category Quality
        ↓
Subcategory Quality
        ↓
Resource Quality
```

Categories include:

* Geological
* Botanical
* Agricultural
* Faunal
* Cultural

These influence:

* ores
* plants
* animals
* dungeon rewards
* monster materials
* artifacts

Quality is geographic rather than random.

---

# 3. Quality System

Quality is stored only as:

```
honorables.quality
```

The numeric value is canonical.

Frontend names are presentation only.

Quality propagates through production.

```
Raw Resource
      ↓
Processing
      ↓
Refinement
      ↓
Crafting
      ↓
Enhancement
      ↓
Final Equipment
```

Higher-quality materials become better substrates for future work.

Quality is intended to compound.

---

# 4. Industrial Framework

Primitive crafting exists only for survival.

```
Crafting Table
```

Produces:

* simple tools
* primitive components
* emergency equipment

Primitive furnaces intentionally lose quality.

```
Ore
    ↓
Furnace
    ↓
Quality degradation
```

Immersive Engineering forms the industrial backbone.

It exists to:

* preserve quality
* automate production
* remove primitive penalties
* enable civilization-scale manufacturing

Industry is not the endgame.

Industry enables the endgame.

---

# 5. Crafting Philosophy

Equipment quality depends on:

```
Material Quality
        +
Player Skill
        +
Traits
        +
Profession
        +
Workshop
        +
Infrastructure
        ↓
Equipment Quality
```

The best gear cannot simply be found.

It must be produced.

---

# 6. Class Framework

Classes provide specialization rather than exclusivity.

A farmer may wield a greatsword.

A warrior simply uses it more effectively.

Traits remain canonical.

Attributes exist primarily as engine-facing values.

Classes guide:

* abilities
* efficiency
* specialization
* economic role
* progression identity

without hard-locking gameplay.

---

# 7. Dungeon Framework

Dungeon generation:

* Dungeon Crawl
* Claustrophobic Dungeons

Respawning:

* Respawning Structures

Loot access:

* Lootr

Loot control:

* LootJS
* KubeJS

Dungeons become renewable expedition sites.

```
Generate
    ↓
Explore
    ↓
Clear
    ↓
Cooldown
    ↓
Respawn
    ↓
Repeat
```

---

# 8. Combat Ecosystem

Each combat mod has a distinct purpose.

## Vanilla

Baseline enemies.

## Brutal Bosses

Elite procedural encounters.

Only scales vanilla mobs.

Represents exceptional specimens.

---

## Born in Chaos

Roaming overworld danger.

Makes exploration hazardous.

Primary ambient threat system.

---

## Legendary Monsters

Handcrafted dungeon and raid encounters.

Provides curated miniboss experiences.

---

## L_Ender's Cataclysm

Major expedition bosses.

Large progression milestones.

Never intended to overlap with Brutal Bosses.

---

# 9. Loot Philosophy

Combat provides:

* monster organs
* catalysts
* relics
* schematics
* biological resources
* crafting ingredients
* research items
* progression tokens

Combat should generally **not** provide:

* best weapons
* best armor
* permanent power spikes

Monsters provide ingredients.

Civilization creates greatness.

---

# 10. Food Framework

Food is generated dynamically through KubeJS.

Effects derive from:

* ingredients
* nutrition
* composition
* `honorables.quality`

Quality primarily scales:

* potency
* duration

Long-term nutrition becomes another progression vector.

---

# 11. Enchanting and Equipment Enhancement

Enchantments remain distinct from item perks.

* **Vanilla/ESO enchantments** represent magical augmentation and are integrated with the quality framework rather than bypassing it.
* **Item perks** are implemented separately (through your custom systems) and remain bound to crafted equipment.
* Enchanting functions as a parallel profession to smithing, realizing magical potential while respecting material and production quality.

---

# 12. Exploration Philosophy

Players should travel because regions matter.

Different regions provide:

* different qualities
* different monsters
* different materials
* different opportunities

Exploration is economically meaningful.

---

# 13. Settlement Loop

The intended gameplay cycle is:

```
Explore
      ↓
Fight
      ↓
Gather Resources
      ↓
Return Home
      ↓
Process Materials
      ↓
Industrial Production
      ↓
Craft Equipment
      ↓
Upgrade Settlement
      ↓
Unlock New Capabilities
      ↓
Explore Further
```

The settlement is the engine that converts exploration into long-term progress.

---

# 14. Canonical Core Systems

The server is built around these interconnected systems:

| System                                 | Purpose                                                                                            |
| -------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Regional Quality Framework**         | Deterministic geographic resource and loot identity                                                |
| **Continuous Quality System**          | Numeric `honorables.quality` propagated through production                                         |
| **Class & Trait Framework**            | Long-term specialization and player identity                                                       |
| **Settlement/Civilization Framework**  | Shared infrastructure as the primary progression object                                            |
| **Industrial Framework**               | Immersive Engineering–based quality-preserving production                                          |
| **Crafting Framework**                 | Equipment derived from material potential and craftsmanship                                        |
| **Dungeon Framework**                  | Renewable expedition sites with controlled loot and respawns                                       |
| **Combat Framework**                   | Distinct roles for ambient threats, procedural elites, handcrafted bosses, and pinnacle encounters |
| **Loot Framework**                     | Materials and progression resources instead of direct power                                        |
| **Food & Nutrition Framework**         | Dynamic nutrition and quality-driven effects                                                       |
| **Enchanting & Enhancement Framework** | Magical progression integrated with quality without replacing production                           |
| **Exploration Framework**              | Region-driven incentives for travel and discovery                                                  |
| **Player Economy**                     | Resource- and service-based trade with no explicit currency                                        |
| **GameStages & Progression Framework** | Binary unlocks layered on top of skills, traits, and infrastructure                                |

## In one sentence

**Honorables is a deterministic, region-driven civilization RPG where exploration, combat, industry, and specialization feed a shared settlement that transforms high-quality resources into ever-greater capability, making production—not loot—the true source of power.**
