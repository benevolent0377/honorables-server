Here's a concise canonical handbook that captures the current state of the Honorables quality framework.

# Honorables Quality Handbook (Canonical)

## Core Philosophy

Quality is the universal measure of production excellence within Honorables.

It represents the cumulative result of:

* Material origin
* Previous transformations
* Craftsmanship
* Production methods
* Player capability
* Infrastructure
* Preservation of potential

Quality is the primary progression axis for equipment and resources. It is not intended to be bypassed by random loot or magical systems.

---

# Quality Range

Quality is stored as a continuous numeric value.

```
honorables.quality
```

The minimum quality is:

```
1.0
```

Quality never falls below 1.0.

The current design target assumes a practical maximum of approximately 5.0.

Frontend rarity labels are presentation only and are derived from the numeric value.

---

# Single Source of Truth

The only authoritative value is:

```
honorables.quality
```

Names such as Common, Exceptional, or Exceptional++ are UI representations and are never authoritative backend values.

---

# Production Philosophy

Quality is not simply inherited from materials.

Every production operation computes a new quality.

Production combines:

## Potential

Derived from:

* Material quality
* Component quality
* Existing item quality
* Inherited inputs

## Realization

Derived from:

* Weighted relevant traits
* Class specialization
* Unlocked perks/passives
* GameStages
* Tool quality
* Workshop capability
* Industrial capability
* Production method
* Temporary modifiers

The result becomes the new:

```
honorables.quality
```

---

# Transformative Operations

Every meaningful transformation recalculates quality.

Examples:

* Crafting
* Reforging
* Tetra modifications
* Blade replacement
* Component replacement
* Advanced repairs
* Industrial processing
* Enchanting operations

The previous quality is an input, not a permanent property.

---

# Quality Storage

After calculation:

```
honorables.quality = final value
```

This value becomes canonical until the next transformation.

No runtime recalculation is required.

---

# Materials

Materials contribute potential.

Higher-quality materials enable higher-quality outputs but do not guarantee them.

Poor production can waste excellent materials.

Excellent production can maximize their realized potential.

---

# Components

For modular equipment, component quality contributes to the final calculation.

Different components may have different weights depending on the recipe.

Replacing components should trigger a full recalculation.

---

# Regional Distribution

Regional quality is determined procedurally.

Sources include:

* Dimension baseline
* Spawn-distance bias
* Voronoi regional capitals
* Category specialization
* Distance falloff
* Border blending

These values influence:

* Ores
* Crops
* Flora
* Fauna
* Mob drops
* Dungeon loot
* Boss rewards
* Generated equipment

---

# Loot Philosophy

Loot should provide:

* Immediate survival
* Production materials
* Archaeological relics

Loot should not bypass production progression.

Most power should come from transforming found resources into superior equipment.

Low-quality finished equipment is intentionally allowed to support early-game survival.

---

# Mob Drops

Mob drops represent biological or structural materials.

Examples include:

* Bones
* Hides
* Chitin
* Silk
* Venom
* Dragon parts

Their quality is determined by regional quality and generation context.

---

# Boss Rewards

Bosses primarily provide deterministic progression materials.

Bosses should reward ingredients and components rather than random best-in-slot equipment.

---

# Smithing

Smithing realizes physical potential.

Final results depend on:

* Materials
* Components
* Traits
* Class specialization
* Perks
* Tool quality
* Workshop quality
* Industrial capability
* GameStages

Blacksmiths may imbue physical item perks.

Examples:

* Balanced Grip
* Heavy Impact
* Reinforced Spine

---

# Enchanting

Enchanting realizes magical potential.

Enchantments should scale using the same realization philosophy as smithing.

Factors may include:

* Item quality
* Player traits
* Class specialization
* GameStages
* Enchanter progression
* Infrastructure

Enchanting complements quality rather than replacing it.

---

# Item Perks

Item perks are separate from enchantments.

Perks are stored on equipment and implemented through Honorables/KubeJS.

Perks may be:

* Passive
* State-triggered
* Event-triggered

Blacksmiths create physical perks.

Enchanters create magical perks.

---

# Player Abilities

Player abilities are separate from item perks.

Abilities belong to the player through:

* Classes
* Skills
* Progression
* Quests
* Passives

Unequipping an item does not remove player abilities.

---

# Enchantments

Enchantments remain Minecraft's standardized magical modification system.

They are distinct from item perks.

Enchantments should integrate with the quality framework and scale with production capability rather than functioning as an isolated power system.

---

# Design Principle

The world provides potential.

The player realizes that potential.

The greatest equipment is not found—it is created through exceptional materials, exceptional craftsmanship, exceptional specialization, and exceptional infrastructure.
